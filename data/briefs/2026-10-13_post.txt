IMF warns Bangladesh faces its slowest economic growth in 38 years.

The IMF projects economic growth will tumble to 3.2% while inflation hits 10.9%.
At the same time, import costs jumped 22% in September as oil and raw materials got pricier.
Bangladesh Bank is stepping in with emergency cash to keep troubled banks and factories open.
Tough choices lie ahead between fighting inflation and keeping the economy moving.

Rising global energy costs and sticky inflation are squeezing Bangladesh's economic engine.
