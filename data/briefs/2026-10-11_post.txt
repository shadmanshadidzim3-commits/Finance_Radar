Banks have excess cash, but factories are not borrowing. Here is why it matters.

Private sector loan growth has stayed below 5% for six months.
Banks are pouring their extra cash into government bills, pushing returns below 8%.
Meanwhile, garment exports grew 6% and worker remittances jumped 13%.
Dollars are arriving, but domestic business confidence remains frozen.

Plentiful cash in bank vaults does not help the economy if businesses are too cautious to borrow.
