Emergency gas buys rushed to save Bangladesh clothing factories.

Severe gas shortages forced garment factories into forced breaks and layoffs.
To stop factory shutdowns, the government approved eight emergency gas cargoes.
At the same time, bank savings interest dropped below the inflation rate.
Savers are losing purchasing power as emergency energy imports expand.

Energy shortages and negative savings returns are squeezing Bangladesh's economic engine.
