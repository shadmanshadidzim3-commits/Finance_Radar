At the same time, factory owners in Chittagong asked to pause gas bills and loan payments.
Gas shortages are stopping production, cutting export earnings, and making dollars scarcer.
That means higher costs for imported food, fuel, and everyday goods ahead.

Energy shortages and dollar scarcity are combining to drive up daily living costs.
