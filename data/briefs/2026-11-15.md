## Top 10 stories

**1. Government plans its debut international sovereign bond issue in early January to meet external debt obligations.**

According to The Business Standard, the finance ministry is planning Bangladesh's first sovereign bond — a debt instrument issued by a national government in foreign currency to raise money abroad. Bangladesh faces rising external debt service, which is the cash required to pay back principal and interest on foreign borrowings. Issuing dollar bonds into a market where the US 10-year Treasury yield is at 5.154% means paying an exceptionally high coupon, sharply raising future fiscal debt burdens.

Downward revisions mean Bangladesh's debt-to-GDP ratio and tax-to-GDP ratio are structurally worse than rating agencies previously evaluated.
