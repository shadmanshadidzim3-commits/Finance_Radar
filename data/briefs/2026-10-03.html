Today's lesson

Concept

Real Effective Exchange Rate (REER)

Nominal exchange rates tell you only how many units of currency trade for another, like Tk 122.95 per dollar. However, businesses buy real goods, not currencies. The Real Effective Exchange Rate (REER) measures the value of a currency against a weighted basket of major trading partners, adjusted for inflation differences between nations.

If Bangladesh experiences 10% domestic inflation while trade competitors like Vietnam see only 3%, Bangladeshi goods become relatively more expensive to foreign buyers even if the nominal exchange rate stays completely flat. In financial terms, a rising REER indicates that a country is losing international export price competitiveness unless its nominal currency depreciates to offset the inflation gap.
