The World Bank warns Bangladesh growth will stay below 4% through 2028.

Global energy costs and troubled banks are slowing our economy down.
July-August fuel imports jumped 11% while foreign debt hit a record $115 billion.
With private loans stalled, banks are parking idle cash in cheap government debt.
Bangladesh has reached a reform agreement with the IMF to secure funding.

Without fixing banking bad loans and energy supply, economic growth will remain stuck in low gear.
