Today's lesson

Concept

The Trade Deficit

A trade deficit occurs when a country's imports (what it buys) exceed its exports (what it sells). Imagine Bangladesh as a giant shop. If the shop buys Tk 1,000 worth of fuel and raw materials but only sells Tk 700 worth of shirts, it has a Tk 300 trade deficit.

To pay that extra Tk 300, the shop must use its savings (foreign exchange reserves) or borrow money. Today's news shows that because fuel prices doubled, our 'bill' grew much faster than our 'sales,' causing the deficit to widen by 31%. This drains our dollar reserves and makes the Taka less valuable.

Test yourselfIf Bangladesh's export earnings stay the same but the price of imported oil drops by 50%, what happens to the trade deficit? (Answer: It narrows or shrinks).

A type of stock that pays a fixed dividend and gets paid before common stock.

Think of them as a hybrid between a loan and a stock.
