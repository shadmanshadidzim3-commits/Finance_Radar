Inflation hits 8-month low, but fuel cash crisis deepens.

July inflation fell to 8.32% as food price growth slowed.
At the same time, BPC requested a Tk 18,699 crore bailout to keep fuel imports moving.
Meanwhile, stock trading in 3 weak finance companies was suspended.

Slower inflation gives relief to shoppers, but state energy debts threaten trade stability.
