**2. The National Board of Revenue recorded a tax revenue shortfall of Tk 43,000 crore as economic deceleration and import curbs slashed VAT receipts.**

A fiscal deficit occurs when sovereign expenditures exceed revenues, forcing the state to bridge the gap through borrowing. When tax collection misses targets by this margin, the government borrows heavily from commercial banks. This creates crowding out—a condition where state debt issuance absorbs bank deposits, leaving less credit for private industry.

**3. The United Nations will decide tonight whether to approve Bangladesh's request to defer its graduation from Least Developed Country status.**

Graduating from Least Developed Country status terminates preferential tariff access under programmes like the EU's Everything But Arms initiative. A deferral preserves cost competitiveness while trade balances are fragile.

**4. Banking experts warned at an industry conference that 80% of non-performing loans represent deliberate looting enabled by governance failures.**

Non-performing loans (NPLs)—loans where borrowers have halted principal or interest payments—paralyse bank capital. Banks must set aside loan loss provisions, which are mandatory cash cushions deducted directly from operating profits. These non-earning assets depress capital adequacy ratios and compel lenders to charge solvent borrowers higher interest rates.
