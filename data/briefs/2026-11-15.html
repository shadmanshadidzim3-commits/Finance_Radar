Today's lesson

Concept

Fiscal crowding-out

Fiscal crowding-out occurs when heavy government borrowing or obligatory state spending absorbs finite national financial resources, leaving less capital available for private businesses or development projects. In today's context, the government must redirect Tk 60,000 crore annually from national tax revenues simply to recapitalise distressed banks. Because tax revenues are already falling short, funding this bank rescue forces the state to slash capital expenditure, as seen in the record-low 6.53% implementation of the Annual Development Programme. When taxpayer money is trapped repairing past bad debts, new highways, bridges, and ports go unbuilt.
