Why are bank loans getting harder to find in Bangladesh?

The government borrowed Tk 16,601 crore from commercial banks in just two months to cover its deficit.
At the same time, high global oil prices pushed fuel import letters of credit up 65%.
When the state takes up the banking system's spare cash, private companies are left waiting in line.

Heavy public borrowing to pay for costly imported fuel is leaving private businesses starved of bank credit.
