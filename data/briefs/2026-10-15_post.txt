A Tk 7,000 crore state bailout won't fix Bangladesh's broken banking system.

State-owned Sonali Bank just loaned Tk 7,000 crore to rescue struggling National Bank.
The central bank promised to cover any losses if things go wrong.
Meanwhile, garment workers are demanding base wages rise to Tk 30,000 to survive inflation.
Every time public money rescues mismanaged private lenders, regular taxpayers bear the ultimate cost.

Bailing out failing banks with public guarantees protects bad managers while shifting financial risk to ordinary citizens.
