Today's lesson

Concept

Regulatory Forbearance

When asset prices crash, financial institutions that lend against those assets often face catastrophic losses. In an orthodox accounting regime, regulators force intermediaries to mark assets to market value and write off bad debts immediately. However, if multiple firms face simultaneous insolvency, strict enforcement can cause system-wide panic and forced fire sales.

Regulators often choose regulatory forbearance — granting temporary exemptions or extending compliance deadlines. We saw this today as BSEC gave stockbrokers until 2030 to resolve margin loan negative equity. Forbearance buys time for firms to earn their way back to health, but it carries a hidden danger: it allows zombie firms to operate, tying up capital and masking the true scale of institutional distress.
