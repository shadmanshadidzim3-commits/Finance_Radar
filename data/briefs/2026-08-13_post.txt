Gas shortages are forcing Bangladeshi factories onto expensive diesel fuel.

Natural gas shortfalls forced leather factories to boost diesel use by 390%.
Burning liquid fuel keeps machines running but spikes production costs.
This extra fuel buying drives up national import bills.
That expands the current account deficit to $1.6 billion.

Physical energy shortages directly inflate factory operating costs and national dollar outflow.
