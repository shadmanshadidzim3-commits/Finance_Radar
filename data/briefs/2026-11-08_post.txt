Why did Bangladesh's trade deficit just jump 31%?

The country's fuel import bill doubled in the first quarter, swallowing valuable foreign currency.

Garment factories in Gazipur and Savar have reopened after wage talks, bringing back critical export revenue.

To keep kitchen costs stable, the central bank is making it cheaper for traders to import Ramadan food items.

Costly imported fuel remains the primary drag on Bangladesh's foreign currency reserves.
