Bangladesh took a $3.1 billion commercial loan to keep fuel flowing.

Oil sits near $104 per barrel and power plants need fuel.
The government secured a $3.1 billion commercial loan to keep gas and oil shipments arriving.
At the same time, the treasury bought back Tk 1,716 crore of local bonds to pump cash into banks.
The taka remained steady at Tk 123.02 per dollar.

Commercial loans keep the lights on today, but debt servicing bills arrive tomorrow.
