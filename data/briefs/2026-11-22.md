**4. Bangladesh Bank is preparing single-borrower lending caps to curb corporate loan concentration across the banking sector, reported The Daily Star.**

Enforcing single-borrower exposure limits forces commercial lenders to diversify their credit risk, which is the possibility of loss resulting from a borrower failing to repay debts. In the near term, corporate giants face tighter liquidity as they lose access to cheap, concentrated credit lines.
