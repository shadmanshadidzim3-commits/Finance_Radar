Why fuel prices just jumped Tk 20 — and what it means for your wallet.

Bangladesh has already spent 45% of its entire yearly energy subsidy budget in just 90 days.
To stop the budget deficit from blowing out, fuel prices were raised.
Now, expect higher bus fares and pricier groceries as transport costs climb.
Meanwhile, foreign debt payments drained $404 million net from reserves in two months.

Energy subsidies ran out early, and household budgets will now absorb the cost.
