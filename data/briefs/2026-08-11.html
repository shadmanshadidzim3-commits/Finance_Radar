Today's lesson

Concept

Moral hazard in financial sector resolution

Moral hazard occurs when a party takes excessive risks because they know someone else will bear the financial cost of failure. For decades, weak non-bank financial institutions in Bangladesh relied on implicit central bank bailouts to stay afloat despite high default rates. When regulators repeatedly rescue insolvent lenders, owners and depositors ignore risk.

Today's decision to declare three NBFIs non-viable and suspend their stock trading breaks this cycle. By forcing shareholders and creditors to absorb losses, the central bank signals that state bailouts are over, restoring market discipline.
