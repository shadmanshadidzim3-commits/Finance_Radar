Why your groceries may cost more even as food bills get delayed.

Commercial banks are paying over 125 taka per dollar to settle energy import bills.
To keep food prices from jumping before Ramadan, Bangladesh Bank is letting importers delay paying for essentials for 90 days.
This buys breathing room today, but those deferred dollar bills will come due all at once in early 2027.

Delaying foreign payment buys temporary price stability, but it creates a foreign exchange debt wall in the spring.
