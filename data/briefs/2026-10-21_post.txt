Bangladesh's quarterly fuel import bill surged 63% as global oil pushed past $110.

Global energy costs are climbing fast. Brent crude reached $110.95 today.
To stop the taka from tumbling, the central bank had to sell $20 million from national reserves.
Meanwhile, unrecorded cash transactions cost the national treasury Tk 933 billion each year in lost taxes.

Costlier fuel imports drain foreign currency reserves, making domestic tax reform an urgent priority.
