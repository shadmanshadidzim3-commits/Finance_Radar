Global oil just surged past $109 — here is what that means for Bangladesh.

Crude oil jumped over 3.7% in a single day as Middle East tensions threaten international fuel supplies.

Bangladesh imports almost all of its petroleum, so costlier oil inflates power tariffs, transport costs, and factory expenses.

At the same time, national import orders surged 22% last month, putting heavy pressure on dollar reserves.

Surging global energy prices directly increase the cost of running factories and everyday living in Bangladesh.
