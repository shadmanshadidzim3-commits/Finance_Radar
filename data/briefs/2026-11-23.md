## The day in one read

The dominant force today is the formal recognition of a solvency crisis in the banking sector. Non-performing loans have reached Tk 6.27 lakh crore, representing 33.8% of total lending. This means one out of every three taka lent by banks is now in default, a level that threatens the stability of the entire financial system.

What changed today is the quantification of the RMG sector's pain alongside a sharp spike in input costs. Unrest has cost the industry $428 million in lost orders, while cotton prices jumped 7.61% in a single day. This creates a severe squeeze where revenue is falling just as raw material costs are rising, leaving manufacturers with little room to absorb the higher wages currently being debated.

Watch the RMG wage board meeting tomorrow for a potential breakthrough or further deadlock. The one number to track is the 124.46 Taka exchange rate. If it breaks 125.00, the cost of importing gas and cotton will accelerate, further straining the country's foreign exchange reserves.
