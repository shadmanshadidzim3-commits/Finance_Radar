The dominant force is the central bank's plan to fully float the taka within a month. This is a clear signal to the IMF that Bangladesh is ready to stop burning reserves to defend the currency, but it sets the stage for a period of high imported inflation.

What changed today is the formal recognition of the banking sector's rot, with the NPL ratio hitting 30.6%.
