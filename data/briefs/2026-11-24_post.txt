Factories are running out of power, and banks are refusing to lend.

A new industrial survey shows factory production contracted as 93% of plants face daily power cuts. At the same time, state banks are sitting on piles of unlent cash because bad loan fears have frozen new lending. With garment exports to Europe down by $678 million, businesses are caught in a severe cash squeeze.

When energy stops flowing and banks stop lending, industrial growth grinds to a halt.
