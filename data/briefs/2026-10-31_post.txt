Oil hits $117 as two major banks report Tk 6,300 crore in losses.

Global crude oil surged past $117 a barrel, raising the bill for power and fuel imports.
Meanwhile, bad loans caused two top private banks to lose Tk 6,303 crore in nine months.
The government is now seeking nearly $3.8 billion in foreign loans to secure energy supplies.

Rising fuel prices and banking stress are testing Bangladesh's dollar reserves.
