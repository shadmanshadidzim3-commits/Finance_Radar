Bangladesh paid back $404M more in foreign debt than it received.

In just two months, Bangladesh paid $699 million to foreign lenders.
Fresh loans brought in only $295 million.
When debt payments outrun incoming loans, dollar supplies shrink at local banks.
That makes everyday imports, from cooking gas to raw materials, harder and more expensive to buy.

Rising foreign debt repayments are draining domestic dollar liquidity.
