Bangladesh paid back more foreign debt this year than ever — while new loans hit a 14-year low.

Bangladesh borrowed the least from other countries in 14 years, but paid back a record $4.49 billion.
Workers abroad sent home $2.85 billion in July, the most in 8 years — but it's still not enough to cover what the country needs to import.
Meanwhile cooking gas just got more expensive again, and US interest rates are climbing just as Bangladesh cut its own.
All of this pulls dollars out faster than they come in — and that's why the taka keeps sliding.

Bangladesh is running low on the dollars it needs, from every direction at once — aid, loans, and now the interest-rate math too.
