Today's lesson

Concept

Debt overhang

A debt overhang occurs when an organization or country carries so much existing debt that it cannot raise new capital or fund profitable new investments. Any new income earned would go straight to paying off old lenders rather than generating growth. Today's data shows Bangladesh's total external debt reaching $78 billion alongside an 11% surge in debt servicing costs, while new foreign loan commitments plunged 37%.

When debt repayments consume an increasing share of foreign currency earnings and tax revenue, the economy is forced to divert cash away from productive infrastructure or fuel imports just to service past debt. This creates a vicious cycle of stagnant investment and slowing economic growth.

An NPL is a loan where the bank stops earning interest and starts risking its original capital.

The gap between what a bank earns on its loans and what it pays to depositors.

NIM is a bank's profit spread — the higher the spread, the more earnings cushion the bank has against bad debts.

Fiscal deficit

The annual shortfall that happens when a government spends more money than it collects in taxes.

A fiscal deficit is like running a household on credit cards when your monthly salary falls short of rent.
