Today's lesson

Concept

The Crowding Out Effect

When the government's borrowing surges—like the 72% jump we saw today—it 'crowds out' everyone else. There is a limited pool of savings in the country. If the government takes most of it, interest rates for regular businesses go up, and many companies simply cannot get a loan at all. This slows down the whole economy because private investment is the main driver of long-term growth.
