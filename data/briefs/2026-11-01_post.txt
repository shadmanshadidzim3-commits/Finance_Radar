Over 100 garment factories shut down today as workers demand higher wages.

Protests have hit Bangladesh's primary export sector, leading the government to form a minimum wage board.
Exports rose 6% over the last four months to $17 billion, but strikes put future deliveries at risk.
Meanwhile, remittances held at $2.87 billion as dollar demand kept the taka at Tk 124.05.
