## The day in one read

Today was a day of operational relief clashing with financial hangover. The single dominant force was the partial restart of the Moheshkhali LNG terminal after a two-week outage, restoring essential fuel supply to an industrial grid that had forced widespread factory shutdowns.

What changed versus recent days is that physical supply bottlenecks began to ease just as real-economy damage surfaced in economic data. While yesterday highlighted factory closures and rising cotton costs, today's data showed July exports falling 0.9% year-on-year, while trade bodies requested stimulus bailouts for 370 distressed garment factories. Meanwhile, the taka depreciated 1.25% in a single session to Tk 123.71.

What to watch next is whether national gas grid pressure recovers sufficiently to restore full industrial capacity. The single number that will confirm whether this crisis is easing is DSE textile sector turnover share—if it drops below 10% of total daily turnover alongside stable factory output, speculative churn will yield to fundamental recovery.
