Bangladesh's taka keeps sliding, gas is running out, and 5,000 people just lost their jobs overnight.

The dollar got more expensive again today — now Tk 122.74 for one dollar.
A huge industrial group shut all 11 of its factories in Chattogram because of raw material and gas shortages — over 5,000 workers idled.
Oil just had its best month in over a year, so fuel and gas bills are about to bite harder.
Meanwhile the stock market actually went up — but only a handful of stocks did the heavy lifting, not the big banks.

When fuel and gas get pricier and the dollar keeps winning, everyone pays more — even on days the stock market looks good.
