Bangladesh just cut interest rates. The taka fell within hours.

Bangladesh Bank lowered its policy rate to 9.5% today to get businesses borrowing again.

But here's the problem: cutting rates makes holding taka less attractive, right as US interest rates are climbing. Money moves to where it earns more.

The taka dropped 1.4% in one session. The central bank's response was to informally cap the dollar rate.

You cannot control the price of money and the price of your currency at the same time. Something has to give.

Watch the gap between the official dollar rate and the street rate. That spread will tell you how this ends.
