Garment wages are up, but a new energy crisis is looming.

Bangladesh just raised the minimum wage for garment workers to 17,000 taka.
While this helps workers, factories are being hit by a 50% cut in power from a major Indian plant.
At the same time, global gas prices just spiked 11%.
Our biggest industry is caught in a massive cost squeeze.

Higher wages are a win for workers, but energy shortages could threaten their jobs.
