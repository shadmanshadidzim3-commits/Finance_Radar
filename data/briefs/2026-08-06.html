Today's lesson

Concept

Moral hazard in government bailouts

Moral hazard occurs when an economic actor takes big risks because they know someone else will pay for the losses if things go wrong. Today, trade bodies asked the government for low-interest bank stimulus loans to rescue 370 failing garment factories. If central banks or governments constantly bail out private businesses during tough times, company owners lose the incentive to build financial reserves or buy insurance against routine business risks like energy shortages. When profits are kept by owners during boom times but losses are paid by taxpayers during crises, economic discipline breaks down.
