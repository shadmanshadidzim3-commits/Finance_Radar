Gas is back in the pipes, but export data takes a hit. 🧵⚡

The Moheshkhali LNG terminal is back online, restoring fuel to gas-starved factories.
But July export numbers show garment shipments fell 0.9% year-on-year.
Meanwhile, 370 struggling textile factories are asking for government loan bailouts.
And the dollar rose to 123.71 taka as import demands mount.

Fixing damaged gas pipelines takes days, but repairing broken company balance sheets takes months.
