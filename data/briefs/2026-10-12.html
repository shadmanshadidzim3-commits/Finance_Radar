Today's lesson

Concept

The Debt Overhang

A debt overhang happens when an entity's existing debt burden is so large that any newly generated earnings go entirely to old lenders rather than funding new investments. When Bangladesh's external debt hits $114.98 billion while economic growth slips below 4%, foreign lenders worry that tax revenue and export proceeds will be swallowed up by past debt servicing. New investors become reluctant to build factories because they anticipate higher future taxes and restricted dollar withdrawals. As foreign direct investment dropped 28%, the sovereign is forced into structural reform programs with the IMF and World Bank simply to refinance maturing debts rather than funding productive new assets.
