Today's lesson

Concept

Credit Risk

Credit risk is the possibility that a borrower will fail to repay a loan. When a bank lends money, it expects to get the principal back plus interest. If the borrower defaults, the bank loses that cash flow.

Today's news shows that 33.8% of loans in Bangladesh are now classified as non-performing. This high level of credit risk threatens the solvency of banks. Solvency means having enough assets to cover all debts.

If too many loans go bad, a bank may not have enough money to pay back its depositors.
