Inflation in Bangladesh just hit an 8-month low of 8.32%.

Cooling food prices helped slow overall inflation in July.
At the same time, foreign trade pressures widened our trade gap to $27.3 billion.
Meanwhile, workers abroad sent 45.6% more remittances early this month.
Stock market trading surged past Tk 1,000 crore on new margin rules.

Slowing inflation provides domestic relief, but energy imports keep foreign exchange balances tight.
