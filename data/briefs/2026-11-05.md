The central bank is defending the exchange rate against a reserve drain—the depletion of foreign currency holdings used to pay for imports. Selling $120 million in a single session indicates that private banks still cannot meet importer demand for dollars on their own.

**3. IEEFA warns rising LNG prices and financing risks are increasing Bangladesh's power subsidy burden.**

The report highlights a growing fiscal deficit—the gap between government spending and revenue—driven by energy subsidies. If LNG prices stay high, the government must either raise electricity prices for factories or borrow more money, which crowds out private lending.
