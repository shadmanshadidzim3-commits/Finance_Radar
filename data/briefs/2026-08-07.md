Following the central bank's policy rate cut, banks lowered deposit yields, pushing the real interest rate deeper into negative territory. A real interest rate is the nominal interest rate minus the inflation rate. When deposit returns fail to match inflation, bank depositors lose purchasing power, driving capital away from savings accounts and into speculative assets.
