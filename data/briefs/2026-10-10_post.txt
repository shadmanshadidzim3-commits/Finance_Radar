Fixing Bangladesh's banks will cost Tk 60,000 crore every year.

The central bank revealed bank cleanup costs will run for 5 to 8 years.
At the same time, the finance minister rejected strict IMF conditions.
That means the government must borrow this massive sum from domestic banks.
When the state takes all the cash, business loans become much harder to find.

Fixing bad bank loans through domestic debt will squeeze credit for private businesses.
