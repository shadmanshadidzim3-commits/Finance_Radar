Today's lesson

Concept

The Crowding Out Effect

When a government runs a fiscal deficit, spending more money than it collects in taxes, it must borrow the difference. If it borrows from domestic commercial banks, it taps into the finite pool of national deposits. Because the government is considered a risk-free borrower, commercial banks prefer buying government Treasury bills over lending to private companies.

In the first quarter of FY27, the Bangladesh government borrowed Tk 24,245 crore from banks. This absorbed available liquidity. Consequently, private entrepreneurs find credit scarce and expensive.

When private businesses cannot borrow, they cancel factory expansions and freeze hiring. In this way, excessive government borrowing directly shrinks private economic investment.
