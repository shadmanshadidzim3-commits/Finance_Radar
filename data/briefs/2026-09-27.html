Today's lesson

Concept

Negative net resource transfer

A country experiences a negative net resource transfer when the money leaving its borders for debt service exceeds incoming gross loan disbursements. In July-August, Bangladesh received roughly $295 million in new external loans from bilateral and multilateral partners. However, over that same two-month period, the country repaid $699 million in debt principal and accumulated interest charges.

That resulted in an immediate net outflow of over $404 million. When repayments outpace inflows, sovereign foreign currency reserves decline unless an offsetting trade surplus or remittance surge covers the shortfall. This forces central banks to restrict import letters of credit.

External debt service

The cash a government or company must pay overseas lenders to cover loan principal and interest charges.

Bangladesh paid $699 million in debt servicing during July-August, causing a net foreign exchange drain.

Debt service is the non-negotiable rent you pay on borrowed capital before spending on anything else.

Concessional financing

Loans granted by multilateral lenders with very low interest rates and extended multi-decade payback periods.

The ADB approved a $100 million housing credit facility on concessional terms to support low-income families.

Concessional debt is development aid disguised as a low-cost, patient loan.

Redeemable preference shares

Special company shares that pay a fixed dividend rate and must be repurchased by the firm at a set future date.

Apex Footwear approved a Tk 500 crore preference share issue to pay down expensive bank loans.

Preference shares act like corporate debt in disguise, offering fixed payouts without voting control.

Net operating cash flow per share

The actual physical cash generated by core operations divided by the total number of common shares.

SK Trims saw this metric swing from negative Tk 0.53 to positive Tk 1.01 despite accounting losses.

Net profit is an accounting opinion, but operating cash flow is hard cash in the bank account.

Policy repo rate

The interest rate commercial banks pay when they borrow short-term funds directly from the central bank.

Bangladesh Bank kept this benchmark steady to evaluate the inflationary fallout of domestic fuel hikes.

The policy rate sets the cost of money for every loan and savings account in the country.
