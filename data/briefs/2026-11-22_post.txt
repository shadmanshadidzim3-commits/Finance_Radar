Garment factory closures have wiped out Tk 5,136 crore in 18 days.

Over 300 garment factories have faced strikes and shut doors, according to factory owners.
International fashion brands have already shifted Tk 3,096 crore in export orders to other countries.
Meanwhile, banks are charging borrowers nearly 6% over deposit rates to protect their own profits.
When export cash dries up, ordinary jobs and the value of our currency take the hit.

Lost garment orders directly threaten Bangladesh's foreign currency reserves and factory jobs.
