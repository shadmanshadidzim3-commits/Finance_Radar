Today's lesson

Concept

Monetary policy transmission mechanism

When a central bank changes its main policy rate (the repo rate), it does not directly change your fixed deposit or personal loan rate. Instead, it sets off a domino effect called the transmission mechanism. First, commercial banks adjust how much they charge each other for overnight loans in the call money market.

Second, yields on government Treasury bills adjust because banks revalue risk-free government debt. Third, commercial banks alter their interest rates on customer deposits and corporate loans to protect their profits. Today in Bangladesh, we saw this mechanism work at high speed: within 24 hours of Bangladesh Bank's policy rate cut to 9.50%, Treasury bill yields and call money rates plummeted across commercial banks.

Jargon decoder

Debt servicing

The cash required to pay back both the original loan amount and interest on time.

Bangladesh made a record foreign debt servicing payment of $4.49 billion in FY26 while foreign loan commitments fell.

Debt service is the mandatory monthly mortgage payment of a country — it must be paid before any money is spent on new growth.

Transmission mechanism

The process through which a central bank policy change moves step-by-step into real economy interest rates.

Yields on Treasury bills and interbank call loans fell immediately after Bangladesh Bank cut its policy repo rate.

Think of the central bank policy rate as the water valve; the transmission mechanism is the plumbing that delivers that water to home taps.

Trade credit

An arrangement where a buyer takes goods today and pays the supplier at an agreed later date.

Polish giant LPP suspended Bangladesh sourcing following $40 million in overdue supplier trade credit disputes.

Trade credit is seller-financed trust — when trust breaks down, trade halts faster than if tariffs were raised.

Deposit mobilization

The process by which banks gather savings cash from individuals and businesses to fund loans.

Eastern Bank teamed up with bKash to launch digital fixed deposits directly inside a mobile wallet app.

Deposit mobilization is a bank gathering its raw materials — without deposits, a bank cannot make loans.
