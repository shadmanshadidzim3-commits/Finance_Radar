Today's lesson

Concept

Gross vs Net Foreign Exchange Reserves (BPM6 Standard)

Foreign exchange reserves represent a central bank's foreign currency holdings used to back liabilities and influence monetary policy. However, financial analysts distinguish between 'gross' and 'net' reserves. Gross reserves count all foreign assets physically held by the central bank.

Net foreign reserves, calculated under the IMF Balance of Payments Manual (BPM6), subtract short-term foreign currency liabilities—such as foreign exchange swaps, IMF drawdowns, and currency swap agreements—from gross holdings. When a country reports high gross reserves ($37.11 billion today) but suffers interbank dollar scarcity, it often indicates that net reserves are substantially lower, leaving less unencumbered liquid dollar balance available for immediate import settlements.
